Narrow windows, with the run list as an overlay.

```sh
brew upgrade --cask orbly
```

The upgrade quits the running app first (the bot gets up to 20 seconds to finish its requests,
and the rest resume on the next start) and reopens it. Config and run history are untouched.

## Narrow windows

- The window can now be as narrow as 600 pixels (it was 960).
- The toggle at the left of the top bar (Cmd+B) hides or shows the run list, and Orbly remembers
  the choice.
- When the window is too narrow for the list and the content side by side (under about 800
  pixels), the list opens over the content instead: the toggle, Cmd+B or typing a search opens
  it, and picking a run, Esc or a click outside closes it.
- Narrow screens stack their cards and rows. Settings shows its sections as icons, with a dot
  for issues, unsaved changes and warnings.

## Fixes

- The status bar stays a level above the content, which now scrolls under it with a soft
  shadow instead of running into it.
- The Homebrew caveats mention connecting through the team hub, and that Orbly keeps using
  `~/.verda` until it is moved.
